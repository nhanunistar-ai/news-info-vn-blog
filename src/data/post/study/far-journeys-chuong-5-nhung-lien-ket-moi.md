> "Toi khong biet ho la ai. Nhung toi biet ho khong phai la nguoi — va ho khong bao gio gia vo la nguoi."
> — Robert Monroe

Den chuong 5 cua _Far Journeys_, Monroe da vuot khoi vung quen thuoc cua cac "cu dan dia phuong" o cac tang gan Trai Dat. Ong bat dau gap nhung loai thuc the hoan toan khac — va nhung cuoc gap go nay bat dau thay doi cach ong hieu ban chat cua y thuc.

### INSPEC La Gi?

Monroe dat cho mot loai thuc the dac biet cai ten "INSPEC" — viet tat cua "Intelligent Species". Day la cach ong goi nhung thuc the phi vat ly khong co lien he lich su nao voi Trai Dat, khong tung la nguoi, va co muc do "hieu biet" ve thuc tai kha xa hon bat cu thu gi Monroe da gap truoc do.

Dieu dac biet la cac INSPEC khong "hien ra" nhu ma quy, khong phat sang la lung, khong co canh hoac nhung dau hieu tam linh. Ho giao tiep va ton tai theo nhung cach rat khac voi bat ky mo hinh ton giao hay tam linh nao ma Monroe tung biet.

Dieu dau tien can noi: Monroe khong tuyen bo INSPEC la thien than, khong phai la nguoi ngoai hanh tinh theo nghia vat ly, khong phai linh hon tiep tuc tu mot nhu muc nao cua to chuc tam linh. Ong mieu ta ho don gian la: cac thuc the voi muc do nhan thuc cao hon, ton tai o cac Focus Level cao, co kha nang giao tiep voi ong — va co su quan tam den ong mot cach cu the.

### Khi Ngon Ngu Khong Cong Tac Nua

De hieu dieu Monroe mo ta trong chuong nay, can quay lai khai niem "Rote" tu chuong 1.

Trong cac cuoc gap truoc voi cac thuc the o cac tang gan, giao tiep con kha "nguoi": ho su dung ngon ngu, hinh anh quen thuoc, boi canh Trai Dat. Ngay ca nhung linh hon chua biet minh chet cung noi tieng Anh hoan hao.

Nhung INSPEC giao tiep hoan toan khac.

Monroe mo ta qua trinh nhu the nay: mot "goi" thong tin duoc truyen den, co chieu sau nhieu tang, con lon va phuc tap hon bat ky cuoc tro chuyen thuong nao. Ong "nhan" no toan bo trong mot khoang khac bat khoa hoc — va ong phai mat nhieu gio sau de "giai ngo" no thanh ngon ngu de ghi chep lai. Giai thich nao cung mac cam giac "mat mat" — nhu the chuyen tu mau sang den trang.

Day la Rote o muc cao hon: khong chi mot goi tu the, ma la nhung "cong trinh kien truc y thuc" phuc hop — goi "toan bo nen van hoa", "toan bo mo hinh vu tru", "toan bo lich su mot loai thuc the" trong mot trao doi tuc thoi.

### "Total Self" — Cai Toi Day Du

Mot trong nhung khai niem quan trong nhat Monroe bat dau hiu het trong chuong nay tu cac cuoc gap voi INSPEC la "Total Self".

Khi chung ta noi "toi", chung ta thuong muon noi cai toi dang ton tai trong than the nay, trong thoi diem nay, voi lich su ca nhan tu khi sinh ra den bay gio.

Nhung theo INSPEC, "toi" do chi la mot phan cua mot cai gi do lon hon nhieu — cai ma Monroe goi la "Total Self" hay doi khi la "I-There cluster". No bao gom:

- "Phien ban" hien tai cua ban — con nguoi dang song cuoc doi nay
- Tat ca cac "phien ban" khac — neu ly thuyet nhieu kiep song la dung, thi la cac kiep song khac; neu khong phai — thi la cac "dan than" cua y thuc vao cac boi canh khac nhau
- "Trung tam dieu phoi" cua tat ca cac phien ban do — mot muc nhan thuc cao hon, ton tai dong thoi voi tat ca, khong bi rang buoc vao bat ky phien ban cu the nao

Monroe khong bat dau voi ly thuyet nay. Ong bat dau voi trai nghiem: o mot muc Focus cao, ong nhin thay "ban than minh" tu nhieu goc do cung mot luc, giao tiep voi "cac phien ban khac cua chinh minh" — va dieu nay tu tu dan den khai niem Total Self nhu mot cach giai thich nhat quan.

### Nhung "Vung" Cu Tru Rieng Biet

Mot dieu nua Monroe kham pha trong chuong nay la cac INSPEC ton tai trong cac "vung" hoan toan khac biet — khong co tiep xuc thong thuong voi cac tang phi vat ly gan Trai Dat hon.

Cuoc gap giua Monroe va INSPEC khong phai la o trung diem. INSPEC "di xuong" gan Trai Dat hon, hay Monroe "di len" cao hon — de co the tiep xuc o mot muc o giua.

Hinh anh nay — khong phai thuy truc tiep, ma la "di den mot diem trung gian" de trao doi — lam nho den cach giao tiep qua bien gioi ngon ngu trong the gioi nguoi: ban khong noi tieng Phap, toi khong noi tieng Viet, nen chung ta gap nhau o tieng Anh la ngon ngu trung gian.

Vung trung gian o day la Focus 27 — ma Monroe mo ta la noi hai "loai" y thuc co the "chung song" du khong tuong dong.

### Khi Cac INSPEC Dat Cau Hoi

Dieu Monroe nhan ra trong chuong nay la: INSPEC khong chi tra loi cau hoi cua ong. Ho cung dat cau hoi lai voi ong.

Va nhung cau hoi cua ho thuong la ve nhung thu ma Monroe cho la hien nhien: Tai sao ong quan tam den viec dam bao su thoai mai vat ly cua co the? Tai sao ong van con giu cac rang buoc tinh cam voi nhung nguoi cu the trong the gioi vat ly? Cac rang buoc do phu vu dieu gi — cho ong, hay cho ho?

Khong phai nhung cau hoi phuc tap. Nhung no la nhung cau ma Monroe thay minh khong co cau tra loi ro rang.

INSPEC khong phong toa hay day Dao. Ho dat cau hoi va cho Monroe tu tim hieu. Day la cach giao tiep cua ho — va cung la dieu Mon roe sau nay nhan ra: moi thu o cac tang phi vat ly cao hon hoan toan thuc hanh "tu do y chi". Khong ai ep ban dieu gi. Ban chi duoc moi.
