> "Ban do khong phai la dia hinh. Nhung khong co ban do, ban cung khong tim duoc duong."
> — Robert Monroe

Khi co the dia ly duoc kham pha, ban do la cong cu dau tien bat ky nha tham hiem nao muon co. Monroe va doi Explorer cua ong, qua hang tram phien lam viec trong nhieu nam, da xay dung duoc mot "ban do" cua nhung gi ho goi la "dia hinh phi vat ly".

Chuong 7 la chuong Monroe trinh bay ban do do chi tiet nhat.

### He Thong Focus Level — Du Lieu Thu Thap Tu Nhieu Nguon

Focus Level khong phai la nhung con so ngau nhien. Chung phan anh cac trang thai y thuc khac biet ve chat, duoc mo ta nhat quan boi nhieu Explorer khac nhau qua nhieu phien rieng biet.

**Focus 22-24**: Cac tang nay chua nhung linh hon "dang mo" hoac boi roi — nhung nguoi chet trong trang thai kho tinh than (nghien, cuong loan, tu tu trong trang thai khung hoang), hoac nhung nguoi chet dot ngot va khong biet minh da chet. Ho ton tai trong mot "trang thai giac mo" van con bi rang buoc boi chuong trinh tam ly cua cuoc song tran gian.

**Focus 25**: Noi ton tai cac he thong niem tin lon cua loai nguoi — thien duong va dia nguc cua cac ton giao khac nhau. Theo Monroe, nhung vung nay "that" theo mot nghia nao do: chung duoc xay dung boi nang luong tin tuong tap the cua hang ty nguoi, va nguoi chet voi niem tin do co the "di vao" va tiep tuc song trong canh quan phu hop voi niem tin cua ho. Mot nguoi Cong giao doan tin vao thien duong co the "thay" thien duong — va no "that" voi ho. Nhung no khong phai la thuc tai cuoi cung.

**Focus 26**: Tang chuyen tiep. Cac thu giu lai cuoi cung — noi nguoi chet dang trong qua trinh buong bo nhung rang buoc con lai voi the gioi vat ly.

**Focus 27**: "The Park" — Co n vien. Day la vung duoc doi Explorer mo ta nhat quan nhat va chi tiet nhat trong toan bo he thong. No la mot noi dep, yim thanh, co cam giac binh yen — noi nhung ai vua roi bo the gioi vat ly co the on dinh va hoc hoi truoc khi "tien len" cao hon. Monroe mo ta day la mot diem tiep nhan duoc "thiet ke" co chu dich — khong phai tu nhien hinh thanh — boi nhung thuc the co nang luc cao hon vi muc dich giai quyet nhu cau cua y thuc trai dat.

**Focus 34-35 va cao hon**: Nhung vung nay bam dau duoc mo ta boi cac Explorer cao cap nhat. Khong co cau truc con nguoi. Khong co canh quan quen thuoc. La nhung "dong luong y thuc thuan tuy" ma Monroe khong co ngon tu dung ngoai tru "nhung thu rat lon" va "nhung thu rat la".

### Cau Hoi Lon Nhat: Nhung Coi Nay Co "That" Khong?

Day la cau hoi ma Monroe tra loi trung thuc nhat co the:

**Lap luan ung ho tinh doc lap vat ly cua cac coi**:
- Nhieu Explorer khac nhau mo ta cung cau truc, cung "vung", cung loai thuc the — ma khong he biet ve bao cao cua nhau
- Thong tin can thiep tu cac tang phi vat ly doi khi xac minh duoc tu cac nguon vat ly ben ngoai
- Trai nghiem cua nguoi ngoai (khong duoc dao tao) trong NDE (near-death experience) khop voi ban do cua Monroe

**Lap luan hoai nghi**:
- Tat ca bao cao deu di qua tam tri nguoi bao cao — khong co cach nao "ghi hinh truc tiep" tu tang phi vat ly
- Cac Explorer co the vo tinh xac nhan bao cao cu thay vi kham pha moi
- Mon roe tu nhan rang cac phien co monitor tin tuong cho ket qua khac voi phien co monitor hoai nghi — co nghia la mong doi anh huong den ket qua

Monroe chon mot lap truong trung gian: nhung coi nay "that" theo nghia la chung co cau truc nhat quan duoc nhieu nguoi co the truy cap — nhung "chat lieu" cua chung co the la y thuc, khong phai vat chat. Theo nghia do, chung "that" nhu giac mo sang suot duoc chia se boi hang ngan nguoi — nhung khong "that" theo nghia la co the chup anh hay do bang may do.

### Focus 27 — Chi Tiet Kham Pha

Trong cac tang phi vat ly, Focus 27 la noi Monroe va doi Explorer dau tu nhieu cong suc nhat de khao sat. Boi vi no co lien he truc tiep den y nghia cua cai chet doi voi con nguoi.

Mot so diem noi bat tu nhieu phien bao cao:

**Co mot cau truc to chuc ro rang**: Khong phai tat ca moi nguoi deu "luu lac" vao co n vien. Co nhung thuc the dong vait tro "nguoi huong dan" — ho giup nhung nguoi moi den nen dinh huong. Ho khong ep buoc. Ho khong phan xet. Ho chi de nghi va de nguoi moi den tu quyet dinh.

**Thoi gian hoat dong khac**: Nhung nguoi "song" trong Focus 27 khong co khai niem thoi gian theo nghia tran gian. Ho co the tiep xuc voi nhung phien ban khac nhau cua ban than ho tu cac "thoi diem" khac nhau cung mot luc.

**Phan "hoc hoi" co thu**:  Co mot qua trinh hoc hoi — on dinh, nhan thuc, hieu nhung gi da xay ra trong cuoc song vua qua, va chuan bi cho buoc tiep theo. Nhung "buoc tiep theo" do la gi, Monroe khong chac chan — nhung ong mo ta no nhu la "tien xuong hoac len, doc theo mot truc gi do ma o day khong co ngon ngu chinh xac".

### Dieu Co Y Nghia Duong Doi

Co mot dieu thuc te tu chuong nay ma khong can tin vao OBE van co the su dung:

He thong Focus Level cua Monroe la mot cach phan loai trang thai y thuc theo chieu doc — khong phai tot/xau, khong phai thong minh/chong mong — ma theo chieu sau va mo rong.

Focus 10 (than xac ngu, tam tri tinh): mot trang thai con nguoi hien dai rat hiem khi dat duoc tu nhien boi vi chung ta khong thuc hanh no.

Focus 12 (y thuc mo rong): mot trang thai ma cac nha thien dinh, nghien cuu, nghe si mo ta la "khi tu tuong bat dau den khong can nen luc".

Focus 15 (vo thoi gian): mot trang thai ma cac va o phu phau thuat mo ta khi ho "nhin thay" toan bo cuoc doi minh trong mot khoang khac ngay ngung tim.

Ban co the khong bao gio OBE. Nhung ban van co the tu hoi: trang thai y thuc binh thuong cua minh la o Focus nao? Va co nhung trang thai khac — phu hop hon cho nhung muc dich khac nhau — ma minh chua kham pha chua?
