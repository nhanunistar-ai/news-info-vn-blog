> "Toi khong biet tai sao no hoat dong. Nhung toi biet no hoat dong. Va di du lieu tiep tuc du vao, toi se tiep tuc dung no."
> — Robert Monroe

Mot trong nhung van de lon nhat voi OBE trong thap nien 1960-70 la khong the tai lap. Monroe co the co trai nghiem OBE. Nhung ong khong the hen truoc duoc ngay mai no se xay ra. Khong the bao ai khac lam theo. Khong co nut bat, khong co cong tac, khong co phuong phap ro rang.

Cho den khi Hemi-Sync xuat hien.

### Nguon Goc Cua Mot Phat Hien Tinh Co

Vao cuoi nhung nam 1950, Monroe — luc do van dang quan ly mot so dai phat thanh — bat dau nghien cuu viec dung am thanh de tro gio ngu. Mot trong nhung phuong phap ong thi nghiem la dung am thanh tan so thap de tao ra trang thai thu gian sau.

Trong qua trinh do, ong nhan ra mot hien tuong la: khi nghe mot tan so nhat dinh, nao cua nguoi nghe co xu huong tu dong dong bo voi tan so do. Khoa hoc goi day la "entrainment" — su co nhan nhip cua nao theo am thanh dau vao.

Nhung buoc phat trien tiep theo dan Monroe den phat hien co ban hon: khi cho hai tai nghe hai am thanh co tan so khac nhau mot chut, nao se "xu ly" su chenh lech do va tao ra mot nhip thu ba — binaural beat — khong ton tai trong thuc te nhung nao "nghe" duoc va dong bo voi no.

Vi du: tai trai nghe 200 Hz, tai phai nghe 210 Hz. Nao xu ly su chenh lech 10 Hz va bat dau hoat dong o nhip 10 Hz — vung alpha, lien quan den thu gian sau va tang cuong to mo sang tao.

Day la nen tang ky thuat cua Hemi-Sync.

### Nhung Focus Level: Ban Do Y Thuc

Monroe khong chi dung Hemi-Sync de giup nguoi ta ngu. Ong dung no de the hien hoa cac trang thai y thuc khac nhau thanh mot he thong co the mo ta, huong dan va tai lap.

He "Focus Level" la ket qua cua hang nam thu nghiem:

**Focus 10**: "Mind awake, body asleep" — Than xac ngu nhung tam tri hoan toan tinh tao. Day la trang thai ma nhieu nguoi goi la "hypnagogic" — ranh gioi giua thuc va ngu — nhung Monroe hoc duoc cach tru lai o do co y thuc thay vi bi cuon vao giac ngu.

**Focus 12**: Trang thai y thuc mo rong. Cam giac gioi han cua "toi" bat dau mo nhat. Theo bao cao cua nhieu nguoi tham gia, nang luc cam nhan ngoai vat chat (ESP) tang manh.

**Focus 15**: "No time". Trang thai vo thoi gian — cam giac ve qua, hien tai va tuong lai khong con tach ro. Rat kho mo ta bang ngon ngu.

**Focus 21**: "Other energy systems" — Bien gioi he thong nang luong khac. Monroe mo ta day la ranh gioi toi da cua y thuc van con trong tren he vat ly Trai Dat.

**Focus 27**: "The Park" — "Co n vien". Noi Monroe mo ta la "vung dem" danh cho nhung ai vua roi bo xac vat ly — mot noi thoai mai, de chiu de on dinh truoc khi "tien len". Nhieu doi Explorer bao cao gap ngot nhau o focus nay va co the xac nhan nhung mo ta dong nhat.

Cac focus o cao hon nua — 34, 35, 49... — bat dau mo ta nhung thu khac han: cac he thuc tai khong co lien he voi Trai Dat, nhung vung y thuc "thua thu" cua cac thuc the phi vat ly da tieng, va nhung dieu khong the dat ten.

### Tai Sao Day Khong Phai Ky Thuat Tam Linh

Day la diem Monroe rat can than phan biet.

Hemi-Sync khong phai am nhac thien dinh. Khong phai chuong tung kinh. Khong phai bai cau nguyen. No la ky thuat tac dong len tan so hoat dong cua nao do thay doi trang thai y thuc — can ban la mot loai "phan mem" cua y thuc.

Monroe khong yeu cau bat ky niem tin tam linh nao tu nguoi tham gia. Nguoi vo than hoan toan co the tham gia chuong trinh Gateway va co trai nghiem OBE. Nguoi tin Chua cung vay. Boi vi Hemi-Sync hoat dong o tang nao vat ly — no khong can ban phai tin vao bat ky thu gi cu the.

Day la mot trong nhung dieu khien Monroe kha thuyet phuc hon nhieu nha tam linh khi: ong dua ra mot phuong phap ma nguoi khac co the thu va tu nhan xet.

### Gioi Han Va Phe Binh

Can noi that: khoa hoc chu liu chua xac nhan hoan toan cac tuyen bo cua Monroe ve Hemi-Sync.

Nhung nghien cuu doc lap ve binaural beats cho thay chung co tac dung that su len trang thai nao — thu gian, tang cuong tap trung, giam lo au. Nhung duoc lu dat gap xuong "Focus 21" va OBE theo kieu Monroe mo ta — do la chuyen khac, chua co nhieu bang chung khach quan.

Co the hieu dieu nay theo hai huong: hoac la Monroe qua tuyen bo, hoac la cac phuong phap do luong y thuc hien tai chua du nhanh ben de nam bat nhung gi thuc su xay ra.

Monroe chon cach tiep can thuc dung: tiep tuc ghi lai nhung gi xay ra, tiep tuc huong dan nguoi khac, tiep tuc thu thap bao cao — trong khi xac nhan rang ong khong co giai thich khoa hoc hoan chinh.

Day la thai do ma — du tin hay khong tin OBE — la thu dang hoc tu ong.

### Dieu Dang Ngu Ve Hemi-Sync

Co mot kich thuoc khac cua Hemi-Sync ma Monroe ky luong ghi lai: tac dong cua no len y thuc nhom.

Khi nhieu nguoi cung nghe cung audio Hemi-Sync trong cung moi truong, ho thuong bao cao nhung trai nghiem co su giao thoa: nhin thay nhau trong trang thai OBE, nhung mo ta dong nhat ve nhung "vung" ho tham hiem, kha nang "chia se" trai nghiem theo thoi gian thuc.

Dieu nay khong the xay ra neu OBE chi la san pham cua cam giac chu quan rieng tung nguoi. No goi y rang hoac co mot "noi" phi vat ly thuc su cac cai ngo co the hop luu, hoac co mot co che nao khac ma khoa hoc chua giai thich duoc.

Monroe khong giai quyet cau hoi nay. Ong chi ghi lai no nhu mot dieu can tiep tuc kham pha — va day la cach ong tiep can moi thu trong cuoc doi: voi su to mo khong so hai.
