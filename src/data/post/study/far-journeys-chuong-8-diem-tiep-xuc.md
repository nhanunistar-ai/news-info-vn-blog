> "Ho dung lai va nhin toi nhu mot thu gi do rat la. Khong phai thu ho so. Chi la thu ho khong ky vong se gap o do."
> — Robert Monroe

Trong qua trinh kham pha cac cõi phi vat ly, Monroe nhan ra mot dieu thu vi: ong khong chi di khao sat, ma con dang bi quan sat.

Den chuong 8 cua _Far Journeys_, Monroe bat dau co nhung cuoc gap go ma ong goi la voi nhung "du khach" — nhung thuc the hoan toan khong co nguon goc tu Trai Dat hay he thong y thuc lien quan den Trai Dat. Ho dung chan o nhung "diem tiep xuc" nhat dinh, va doi khi, ho to mo ve ong.

### Ai La Du Khach?

Cac du khach ma Monroe gap khong giong nhu nguoi ngoai hanh tinh trong cac bo phim vien tuong. Ho khong co dia bay, khong co the xac (it nhat la trong khoanh khac giao tiep), va hoan toan khong co quan diem chung nao ve the gioi vat ly nhu chung ta dang biet.

Ho duoc mo ta giong nhu nhung "khoi y thuc" dang di ngang qua cac he thong thuc tai. Mot so nguoi trong so ho dang tren mot hanh trinh dai ma muc dich vuot qua kha nang ly giai cua ngon ngu.

Khi Monroe gap ho, ho thuong xuyen the hien su ngac nhien — khong phai vi ong dang so, ma vi ong la "mot phan cua he thong Trai Dat" dang di dao vung ngoai vi, dieu ma duong nhu khong thuong xuyen xay ra. Ho nhin ong nhu mot sinh vat vat ly (hay y thuc lien ket chat che voi vat ly) bi lac vao mot vung khong gian khac.

### Van De Giao Tiep: Khi Hinh Anh Tro Nen Kho Hieu

Giao tiep voi nhung du khach nay la mot thach thuc lon. Khi Monroe dung "Rote" (nhung goi thong tin tuc thoi) de truyen hay nhan, ong nhan thay rang nhung thong tin tu nhung thuc the khong co lich su Trai Dat vo cung kho hieu.

No giong nhu viec giai ma mot ngon ngu ma ban khong co bat ky tu dien hay kinh nghiem van hoa nao de tham chieu. Khi ho truyen mot "hinh anh" hay mot "cam giac", bo nao cua Monroe phai co gang tim mot thu gi do tuong duong trong the gioi vat ly de dien dich. Ket qua thuong la nhung hinh anh ky di hoac nhung hieu lam co ban.

Dieu nay mo ra mot cau hoi quan trong ve ban chat cua viec hieu: Y thuc cua chung ta phu thuoc bao nhieu vao cac mo hinh the gioi vat ly de giai thich thuc tai? Neu chung ta gap mot thuc tai khong co khong gian, khong co thoi gian, khong co "len-xuong-trai-phai", thi lam sao chung ta co the hieu duoc?

### Monroe Tro Thanh Cau Noi

Mot chuc nang bat ngo ma Monroe phat hien ra trong cac cuoc gap nay la ong doi khi dong vai tro la mot "diem trung chuyen" — mot loai cau noi giua cac he thong.

Cac thuc the phi vat ly khac co the dung "tan so" cua ong de khao sat he thong Trai Dat. Ho co the gui mot tin nhan hoac mot "cam nhan" qua ong. Day la mot qua trinh doi hoi su dong thuan tuyet doi. Monroe khong "bi chiem huu" hay "bi nhap". Ong van hoan toan y thuc va co the dung qua trinh nay bat cu luc nao.

Tuy nhien, no doi hoi muc do kiem soat y thuc cuc cao. Viec cho phep mot thong tin/nang luong khac chay qua he thong cua minh ma khong bi no lam bien dang hay cuon di la mot ky nang phai ren luyen thong qua nhieu gio trong trang thai OBE co kiem soat.

### Lien He Hien Tai: Khi Y Thuc Giao Tiep

Du ban khong tin vao OBE hay nhung thuc the tu vu tru, chuong nay de lai mot bai hoc sau sac ve su giao tiep.

Chung ta thuong mac dinh rang su hieu biet la de dang giua nhung nguoi noi cung mot ngon ngu. Nhung neu chung ta nhin giao tiep nhu viec truyen mot "Rote" (goi thong tin chua day cam xuc, kinh nghiem, niem tin), chung ta se thay su dien dich la dieu kien can, nhung cung la noi de xay ra loi nhat.

Moi nguoi chung ta dang giai ma the gioi qua mot "bo loc" rieng duoc xay dung tu toan bo cuoc doi minh. Hieu duoc dieu do, viec hieu sai hay khong the hieu duoc y nguoi khac khong phai la loi co y, ma la dac tinh cua viec nam giu nhung bo loc khac nhau. 

Monroe da phai hoc cach "mo bo loc" de tiep nhan cac thong tin phi vat ly ma khong danh gia chung dua tren chuan muc cua Trai Dat. Co le, chung ta cung can hoc cach do trong cuoc song hien tai.
