> "Toi biet nhieu hon khi toi bat dau. Toi cung hieu ro hon la minh khong biet bao nhieu."
> — Robert Monroe

"Segue" — tieng Latin, co nghia la "theo sau", duoc dung trong am nhac de chi chuyen tiep khong cat ngat giua hai phan — la ten Monroe chon cho chuong ngan nhat va co le la sau sac nhat cua _Far Journeys_.

Day khong phai la chuong kham pha. Day la chuong dung lai.

### Nhung Gi Da Biet

Den cuoi Phan I, Monroe da tich luy duoc mot khoi luong du lieu lon:

Tu doi Explorer, ong co hang tram bao cao trai nghiem co giam sat, co ghi am, co doi chieu. He "Focus Level" da duoc xay dung va co the day cho nguoi khac. Chuong trinh Gateway dang chay va thu hut hang nghin nguoi tham gia moi nam.

Tu cac cuoc gap voi INSPEC, ong co mot khuon kho mo — con rat nhieu khoang trong — de hieu vy sao co nhieu "loai" ton tai phi vat ly va tai sao chung giao tiep theo nhung cach rat khac nhau.

Tu chính nhung trai nghiem ca nhan, ong biet rang co nhung "vung" phi vat ly voi cau truc nhat quan — Focus 27, cac Tang giao dich, cac vung cu tru cua cac loai thuc the khac nhau — va rang cac cac vung nay duoc mo ta tuong tu boi nhieu nguoi khac nhau.

Day la nhung gi "biet". Nhung Mon roe dung lai o chuong 6 vi co nhieu thu quan trong hon: nhung gi chua biet.

### Nhung Cau Hoi Chua Co Loi Giai

**Y thuc la gi?**

Day la cau hoi trung tam cua toan bo cuon sach — va Monroe nhan ra rang sau muoi lam nam nghien cuu, ong van chua co cau tra loi thoa man.

Hemi-Sync tac dong len nao de thay doi trang thai y thuc. Nhung dieu do khong noi gi ve ban chat cua y thuc la gi. No chi noi y thuc co the o nhieu trang thai khac nhau. No co phai la san pham cua nao? Hay nao chi la "anten" de y thuc hoat dong? Neu y thuc tach khoi nao trong OBE, lieu no van la "minh" hay la mot thu gi khac?

**Cai chet la gi?**

Monroe da gap rat nhieu nguoi "chet" trong cac trai nghiem OBE. Nhung "chet" trong ngu canh do co nghia gi? Ho thay doi, ho tien len, ho on dinh trong nhung vung khac nhau cua thuc tai phi vat ly — nhung khong co ai bien mat hoan toan. "Bien mat" co con la tuy chon khong?

**Thuc tai la gi?**

Trong thi nghiem, cac Explorer co the "di vao" cac vung phi vat ly va bao cao nhung thu nhat quan voi nhau. Nhung lieu nhung vung do ton tai "ngoai kia" doc lap voi y thuc cua nguoi kham pha — hay chung la nhung phong chieu cua chinh tam tri? Va neu la phong chieu — phong chieu tu cai gi?

**Muc dich la gi?**

Monroe bat dau nghi rang co mot "muc dich lon hon" ma ong — va co the la toan bo nhan loai — dang tham gia. Nhung muc dich do la gi? Ai hoac cai gi dat ra muc dich do? Va tai sao Trai Dat lai o trung tam?

### Khi Khoa Hoc Va Trai Nghiem Gap Nhau

Chuong 6 cung la noi Monroe tra loi thang thung nhat cho nhung phe binh khoa hoc ve cong viec cua minh.

Ong nhin nhan:

- Cac phuong phap nghien cuu cua ong khong dat chuan khoa hoc chinh thong
- OBE khong the tai lap duoc theo nghia khoa hoc nghiem ngat
- Cac bao cao cua Explorer khong the xac minh khach quan
- Ong co the dang bi danh lua boi chính tâm tri minh

Va ong vẫn tiep tuc. Khong phai vi he khong quan tam den bat luan. Ma vi:

"Dieu tot nhat toi co the lam la ghi chep lai chinh xac nhung gi toi quan sat, toi trai nghiem va cac explorer toi cua toi bao cao. Toi se de lai cho nhung nguoi khac — can trong hon, thong minh hon, voi thiet bi tot hon — phan tich y nghia cua no."

Day la su khiem ton thuc su: biet rang minh khong co cau tra loi cuoi cung, nhung van ghi chep lai vi du lieu co gia tri — ngay ca khi ban than khong biet gia tri chinh xac la gi.

### Chuan Bi Cho Phan II

Chuong 6 ket thuc bang mot canh bao nhe:

Phan I, Monroe noi, la nhung thu co the kiem tra, co the cap, co the giang day. Nhung Phan II thi khac. Nhung thu trong Phan II la nhung thu ong kho co the dam bao bat ky ai co trai nghiem tuong tu. No la nhung thu ong gan nhu chac chan la that — nhung no cung la nhung thu xa hoan toan khoi bat ky tham chieu thuong nao.

Day la loi moi goi tiep tuc doc. Va cung la loi canh bao: neu ban lo luong ve nhung thu trong Phan I, ban nen chuan bi tinh than ky truoc khi buoc sang Phan II.

Boi vi nhung thu sap toi — ban do cac coi, khai niem Loosh, the Gathering — se dat cau hoi khong chi ve y thuc ca nhan. Ma ve ban chat cua toan bo su ton tai nguoi.
