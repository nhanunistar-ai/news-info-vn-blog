> "De xay mot toa nha moi, doi khi ban phai phai dung min de phang phang toa nha cu. Dieu do chua bao gio la de chiu ca."
> — Robert Monroe

Neu nhu Chuong 12 danh guc Monroe ve mat cam xuc, thi Chuong 13 ("Shock Treatment" - Lieu Phap Soc) la nhung phat sung the the pha huy tiep theo vao nhung he thong niem tin (Belief Systems - BS) cot loi the the ma ong dang con bau viu.

Ban co the the phan nao tin rang the the ban rat mo mang. Nhung chi khi ban bi nem vao nhung thuc tai the ngau nhien hoan toan the phan bac the niem tin, ban moi thay the vung cua no the the the. Monroe da the chiu "Lieu phap soc" the tu the INSPEC.

### Su Xay Dung Cua He Niem Tin (Belief System)

Khi di qua the cac The Focus (Focus 25, 26...), Monroe da tung khao sat nhieu the the niem tin the cua the the the chet (cac Belief System Territories). Day la the nhung noi nguoi ta ton tai sau chet dua the niem tin ton the hoac xa the ho co: co thien the ho, co the dia nguc the day the, co the noi moi nguoi chi ban luan triet the...

Tuy nhien, phai hieu la tat the no la **gia tao** xet tren dien rong vu tru. Do the niem tin cua chinh nhung y thuc do tu phat ra nang luong the cau the canh. Chung tu the dong bang trong the the the, tro thanh "ao anh" cua chinh chung. 

"Soc" o day xay ra khi cac INSPEC thuc hanh mot thu the: Cho Monroe thay rang tham the nhung hieu the the the hien tai cua ong ve "the gioi the OBE" ma ong the the, the the cung the the the mot the niem tin (BS) khac! 

### Thuc Han Lieu Phap Soc

De thao go the "ao anh" cuoi the cua ong, INSPEC dua Monroe toi nhung thuc the the the khong the dien the hieu the nua. 

- Mot trong nhung thi the la the y thuc ma Monroe "thay" the nhung luong y thuc the qua the. Thay the vi the phien ban "Total self" hay "nguoi", ho khong co hinh the, khong co tieng noi, the the thoi gian khong ton the the truoc the sau ma chay the song song.
- Mot trai nghiem soc khac the the hien tuong the the the "thu tuc" the y thuc chet the sinh ra de the thanh the nhan the. Su de the cua moi nguoi hoan toan la su huyen hoac cua viec lua the the boi vi ban the y thuc hoan toan khong co quyen rieng. 

Cach ma INSPEC giao tiep the la dot nhien the "nho" ong the vao the trai nghiem nay the the the ma khong co "loi huong dan". Nhu the nhay vao dai duong khi khong the boi, ban the phai chiu su so hai tot the the de the biet cach thao got the xac the. Monroe thuong "bat ngo" boi thuc the The the cua the the, so hai the, va cuoi cung thu nhan rang minh da dat ky vong.

### Tai Sao Phai "Soc"?

Viec the xac nhan thuc qua the bao han the rat thu vi the. Nhung theo the Monroe the nhan sau khi qua the "soc", The INSPEC khong the thuc the hanh ac, ho dang dung cach nhanh nhat de "Thao got the xac" (Un-BS). 

Monroe goi do la qua trinh Un-BSing.
De tu the nhan the, y thuc the phan tich, ta the the mo ta va co tim ra nghia the, nhung viec do se the tro thanh chiec luoi the cua the BS the. "Soc" la cach dua ban den the diem the noi ban khong The Dung Ly Tri The Nua. Ban buoc phai buong bo hinh anh cua the gioi de thu thap the thuc the.

### Hanh Trinh Ve Voi Cuoc Song 

Dieu thu vi trong "Lieu phap Soc" la sau cung the chung khong di theo con the huy diet, ma mang toi hieu biet cuc ky sang the. 

De khao sat su rong lon cua Y thuc the the the thuoc the the xac the nay the, chung ta the phai de cho nhan the the the the roi rung di moi cai the the. De lam the, thi moi su khong the hieu cua thuc tai hien tai chinh la su an the the the. Nhung su viec khung the, nhung hoang the, hay nhung the the soc trong cuoc the ban khong phai the su phan tich, the co the la do the INSPEC cua chinh the dang dat "Lieu phap soc" the cho the the the the the BS.
