> "Cai toi dang nhin ban bay gio khong phai la toan bo toi. No chi la mot giot nuoc duoc dua ra tu dai duong de tro chuyen cung ban ma thoi."
> — Loi mot INSPEC gui Robert Monroe

Sau nhieu cuoc du hanh trong cac cõi phi vat ly, Monroe nhan ra ong can su ho tro de di tiep. Su vo tan cua cac Focus Level co the tro nen ap dao va tham chi boi roi. Chuong 10 danh dau su thay doi: Monroe bat dau hinh thanh mot "tinh ban" dai han voi mot thuc the tu tang cao hon (INSPEC).

### Nguoi Ban Khong Ten

Monroe khong bao gio dat mot ten cu the, nghe co ve "ton giao" cho huong dan vien cua minh. Ong goi thuc the nay la "nguoi ban" hoac don gian la mot INSPEC cu the nao do da danh thoi gian de ho tro ong.

Nguoi ban nay dong vai tro khac biet so voi cac thien than bao ho hoac linh hon huong dan trong cac van ban tam linh truyen thong:

- **Khong bat buoc:** INSPEC nay chi hien dien khi Monroe di vao mot tang nhat dinh, hoac khi co su dong y giua hai ben. Ho khong thao tung ong tu tren cao.
- **Su kien nhan phi gioi han:** Khong co thai do len mat day doi hay phan xet. Nguoi ban nay dong vai tro nhu mot chiec guong, cung cap nhung goc nhin moi chu khong dua ra "chan ly the the".
- **Lien ket Rote:** Giao tiep luon duoc thuc hien thong qua cac goi thong tin tuc thoi (Rote) cuc ky phuc tap, bat buoc bo nao Monroe phai hoc cach xu ly thong tin nang hon rat nhieu the tich binh thuong.

Thong qua INSPEC nay, Monroe bat dau hieu hon ve ly do tai sao ong duoc phep (hoac co kha nang) kham pha xa nhu vay vao luc ma it nguoi o the gioi hien dai lam duoc mot cach he thong.

### Khai Niem Ithaca — Niem Khao Khat Tro Ve

Trong mot cuoc gap go, nguoi ban nay truyen cho Monroe mot Rote chua dung mot cam xuc vo cung manh me, thu ma ong hinh tuong hoa thanh "Ithaca". 

Cai ten duoc lay tu su thi Homer: que huong ma Odysseus muon tro ve sau nhieu nam phieu bat. 

Trong the gioi cua Monroe, Ithaca tuong chung cho **nguon goc** thuc su cua moi y thuc. No khong phai la Trai Dat. No la diem khoi dau cua cai goi la "Total Self" (Cai toi toan ven) truoc khi "chia tay" va gui cac manh nhan thuc (nhu con nguoi Monroe) vao the gioi vat ly de trai nghiem.

Monroe mo ta rang cam giac khao khat duoc tro ve "Ithaca" la cuc ky sau sac. Nhieu linh hon tren Trai Dat, du song mot cuoc doi thanh cong va hanh phuc, van luon mang mot noi nho mong man mac khong the ly giai ve "nha" - ma khong biet thuc su "nha" la o dau. Nguoi ban INSPEC giup ong hieu rang, noi khao khat ay chinh la luc keo cua Ithaca, huong y thuc ve lai ban the.

### Chung Ta Thuc Su La Ai?

Cuoc tro chuyen voi INSPEC nay dua Monroe den cho giai quyet cau hoi ban the cot loi: Chung ta la ai?

Chung ta khong phai la co the nay. Chung ta tham chi cung khong phai la nhung tinh cach hay so thich cua kien nay. Theo thong diep nhan duoc: Chung ta la nhung "tham do" ma Total Self dung de trai nghiem cam giac, xuc giac, va cac loai nang luong ma chi co trong moi truong nhu Trai Dat (truong thoi gian - khong gian).

Khi mot nguoi chet, ho khong bien mat, ho mang "du lieu" cua toan bo kiep song nay tro ve, gop vao tong the lon hon. Nguoi ban INSPEC nay co the, chinh la mot Total Self dang nhin xuong va giao tiep voi mot "tham do" cua mot he thong khac (Monroe), hoac tham chi... co the chinh la Total Self cua Monroe trong tuong lai hay mot khong gian vo huong? Monroe de ngo kha nang nay.

### Moi Lien He Voi Cuoc Song

Goc nhin ve Ithaca mang lai mot tac dong xoa diu cuc lon. Neu chung ta thuc su chi la nhung du hanh gia dang "di lam nhiem vu" de thu thap kinh nghiem va trai nghiem, thi nhung kho khan hay ton thuong ma ta doi mat tren the gioi nay co the duoc nhin nhan nhu cac bai tap, cac thu nghiem hon la mot ban an khong the tranh.

Y thuc ve viec ban co mot "Nha" dich thuc, khong phu thuoc vao viec ban dang o noi nao hay thuoc ve the che xa hoi nao tren Trai Dat, co the la nguon dong luc sau the de ban the song vung vang va nhe nhang hon trong cuoc doi vat ly.
