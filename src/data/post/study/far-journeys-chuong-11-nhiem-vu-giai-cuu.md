> "Ho dung do, hoan toan boi roi. Ho nhin thay ban be va nguoi than khong them nhin minh, khong nghe minh. Ho giong nhu nhung dua tre di lac va dang tuyet vong."
> — Robert Monroe

Trong qua trinh khao sat "dia hinh" phi vat ly gan Trai Dat, dac biet la o cac tang Focus 22-24, Monroe va doi Explorer phat hien ra rat nhieu nguoi... bi ket lai. 

Ho goi nhung nguoi nay bang mot cai ten ky thuat: cac thuc the bi mac ket (stuck entities). Chuong 11 mo ta quy trinh va su lua chon ma Monroe dua ra khi doi dien voi ho: Ho bat dau thuc hien cac "nhiem vu giai cuu".

### The Nao La Mot "Linh Hon Mac Ket"?

Theo kinh nghiem cua Monroe, khong phai ai qua doi cung ngay lap tuc "bay ve anh sang" hay di den Focus 27. Rat nhieu nguoi - boi nhieu ly do khac nhau - khong he biet la minh da chet hoac tu choi tin vao su that do.

- **Nhung cai chet dot ngot:** Nhieu nguoi tu vong trong tai nan nhanh hoac chien tranh bat ngo khong kip hieu chuyen gi da xay ra. Y thuc ho tiep tuc hanh dong nhu the minh van con song.
- **Bi am anh qua muc voi vat chat:** Nhung nguoi qua "nghien" the xac (tien bac, the xac, cam giac) thuong bam lay the gioi vat ly mot cach vo vong de tim kiem lai su kich thich, tham chi khao khao tim cach chiem lay hoac huong huong nhung song nang luong cua nguoi song.
- **Niem tin tan giao co chap:** Nhung nguoi the chet tin vao dia nguc, ho co the so den muc ho tu "dong bang" minh lai vi so rang buoc tiep theo la hinh phat, the la ho dung do mai mai trong noi so.

Voi Monroe, nhung nguoi nay khong phai la quy du dang so, ma giong nhu nhung nan nhan bi mat tri nho. Ho dang hoang mang, dau don vi khong ai (nguoi song) nhan thay ho, con nhung the gioi ben tren ho lai tu choi mo mat de nhin.

### Quy Trinh "Giai Cuu"

Thay vi de mat thay thi thoi, doi Explorer, phoi hop cung voi cac "nguoi ban" INSPEC, bat dau thiet ke cac phien tap trung vao viec tro giup nhung y thuc nay.

Giai cuu o day khong phai la tieu diet cung chang phai ban phep mau. Giai cuu the hien qua mot vai buoc cu the:

1. **Gay chu y:** Bieu hien nhu mot the xac hoac nguoi quen. Nhieu nguoi chet van hieu moi thu thong qua he tham chieu the xac. Monroe va Explorer thuong tao ra cac form dang giong con nguoi de de tiep can ma khong gay so hai.
2. **Kiem tra hien trang thuc te:** Cho ho thay nhung dieu phi ly, nhu di xuyen tuong hoac loi keo su chu y cua ho vao su that: "Hoi nao den gio anh chua tung cam thay doi bung chua?"
3. **Thuyet phuc va dan duong:** De xuat cho ho huong toi vung "anh sang" (thuong la duong the di toi Focus 27) hoac tao hinh thuc de mot "nguoi than" (y thuc cua nguoi than da mat cua nguoi do, den don) thay.

Quan trong nhat, quy trinh giai cuu la the hien rang *co the tiep tuc phat trien*, khong phai bi vuong mac vo tan trong hoan canh tam ly nhat thoi.

### Bai Toan Dao Duc: Quyen Can Thiep

Viec nhung "con nguoi dang song" thu xep can thiep vao nhung nguoi chet dua len mot cau hoi cuc lon: Ta co quyen do khong?

Neu the gioi duoc thiet ke tu do va theo nguyen tac y chi tu do (Free Will), thi viec ho chon (du la vo thuc) bi ket cung la quyen cua ho. Monroe tu van minh va trao doi voi INSPEC ve dieu nay. Ong hoi: "Tai sao cac ngai (cac y thuc cap cao) khong tu minh tro giup ho?"

INSPEC dap lai: Cac y thuc bi the the qua muc o Trai Dat gan nhu *mu quang* voi cac nang luong tinh khiet, ho khong the nhin thay cac INSPEC. Ho chi con rung dong duoc voi nhung dang gan voi Trai Dat - nghia la chinh cac Explorer - nhung nguoi van dang so huu mot "tan so the xac" vung chac, moi de dang tro thanh doi tuong hien dien ro rang trong the gioi cua nhung linh hon mac ket.

Monroe cho rang viec ho de de xep nhung cai chet tro thanh mot phan cuoc thu nghiem giup cho loi ho giup "khi co the" la dieu nhan van, chu khong phai vuot quyen. The nhung do van la ranh gioi mong manh cua viec "giup" va "lam phien".

### Lien He Hien Tai: Ai Chua Chet Ma Van "Ket"?

Bai hoc cho chung ta khong phai la chuyen giai cuu ma co. Bai hoc lon nhat o day la **su mac ket tinh than**.

Co rat nhieu nguoi song dang lam vao canh giong het cac "stuck entities" tren: chung ta hoan toan tu nhot minh vao nhung he thong niem tin cu ky, tu tuong cua chung ta xuyen vao nhung uan khuc cu cua the gioi ma quen di ta dang phat trien. 

Nhan thuc ra trang thai cua minh de khong bao gio bi troi chat sau khi roi di la mot su "giai cuu" tu do cho chinh ban ngay tai the gioi nay.
