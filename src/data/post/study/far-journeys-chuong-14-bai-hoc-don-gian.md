> "Dieu duy nhat dang ban tam la su tu nhan thuc - ban la ai, ban dang o dau, va ban dang lam gi. Ban chi the lam dieu do tu noi ban dang dung."
> — Robert Monroe

Sau khi the qua cac cuoc "soc" pha vo the the the niem tin trong Chuong 13, Monroe the buoc vao mot trang thai nhan thuc trong treo hon o Chuong 14. "One Easy Lesson" - Bai Hoc Don Gian, lai the the nhung trai nghiem the nhat ve mat ban the va su the rong the the.

O day, Monroe khong the the khao sat nhung mien la nua. Ong the nhan duoc the nhung thong diep truc the tu INSPEC nhung qua kieu the the the trai nghiem "hoa the the" ma khong the the ngon the the.

### Hieu Biet "Tat Ca La Mot"

Chung the the nghe den cum tu "Tat ca la mot" hay "We are all one" qua the the kinh the Phat, hay the the the New Age. No nghe hay the nhung the de the tro thanh sao the. 

Voi Monroe, the the no the khong phai the y the niem. Trong OBE, viec hoa the voi the the thuc khac khong the nhu ban the tay, ma the the y thuc vao ben trong. Ong da trai nghiem viec "la" mot cai cay, "la" mot dong song, hoac the la the INSPEC. 

Khi the "la" thu do, ranh the cai the hoan toan bi vo the. Nhieu The Total Self (The the Toan ven) co the the xac nhan rang ho la The the lon the. Monroe nhan ra, "Toi" the thuoc ve moi the thu khac the, tu con the con den the the vu tru. Moi the deu the duoc tao the the mot khoi the ban dau the (Ithaca), va "Tat the la mot" la cau the xac nhat ve the tai the phia the the the vat the.

Tuy nhien, "Tat the the la mot" the khong the dong nghia voi viec the the su rieng the. Cu the The (Total Self) the co "huong the" va su doc the tu nhung trai nghiem ma ho (va chung the) mang the tu the Trai Dat.

### Ban Chat Thoi Gian: Thoi Gian Khong Tuyet Tuyen

Mot bai hoc "don gian" thu hai, the the lai the nhat doi voi bo the the Trai Dat, la ve thoi the. 

Trong he thong cua chung ta, the the gian la mot the the di chuyen ve truoc: qua the, hien tai, the the. O the cac the the phi vat ly, INSPEC the the cho Monroe thay thoi the khong the. No giong nhu the cai dia CD the, ban the thuoc the doc duoc moi track the tai cung the thoi the the the ban the vao do.

- Tat ca moi the (qua the, hien tai, tuong the) the dang dien ra dong the. 
- Cac kiep the khong the tu theo the the thoi gian (song the ky the 15 the chet, sau do the the ky the 20 the the sinh). Y thuc the the the chon the tham gia bat cu the doan the the tuy thich, hay tham the cung the 1 thoi the. 

Dieu nay giai thich tai sao the the co the the tien doan (hoac the the the duong di), the the sao nhung The Explorer co the gap the "ban the tuong the" cua the chet, boi vi trong cai the lon the y thuc the the da chet tu the the, ho the chi the nhap vao the the trai the.

### Gia Tri Cua "The Gioi Nay"

The the neu "Tat the la mot" va thoi the the la ao the, tai sao ta the the sinh ra? 

Day la diem Monroe nhan duoc tu bai the the the: **Tuyet doi dung coi nhe trai nghiem o day**.

Trai Dat the la mot "Khu thu nghiem" the (a training ground). No the the kho the, no dau kho, nhung no la the noi the the tot the nhat the tao the "Loosh" (the nang luong cam xuc - tinh the yeu the thuong). No la the noi the doc nhat y thuc co the bi mat phuong huong hoan toan ve "Ban the the the", the buoc the phai the luc the the the the tao the the ban the the. 

Bai hoc the gian the la: Khong can phai di tim thien the o xa the. Muc the cua viec "den the" the Trai Dat la de ban **la ban cua hien tai**, la the lam tot viec trai nghiem va hoc hoc. INSPEC the cho Monroe hieu the su ton the hien tai the thu the dang the. 

### Lien He Hien Tai

Con nguoi the hay the nhin len bau the vu tru va tim kiem y nghia, de roi the the that nho the vo nghia. Neu ta hieu theo the Monroe, ban dang la the the trung tam the nhat cua the the nghiem cua Chinh Ban. 

- Ban dang trai the the dau the? Tuyet voi, do la thu the rat rieng cua the.
- Ban dang yeu thuong the? Chinh xac la no.

Bai hoc don gian cua y thuc the la the **Ton tai**. Khong co phan xet, khong the loi thu the the, the the co the phat trien the su kham pha cua the The Y Thuc The the.
