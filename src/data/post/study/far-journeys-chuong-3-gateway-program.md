> "Chuong trinh nay khong day ban OBE. No tao dieu kien de OBE co the xay ra. Su khac biet rat quan trong."
> — Robert Monroe

Nam 1974, Monroe va cac cong su cua ong mo cua mot thu gi chua tung ton tai truoc do: mot chuong trinh dao tao chinh thuc de kham pha y thuc ngoai vat ly — co giao vien, co tai lieu, co co so vat chat, co lich trinh — nhung hoan toan khong yeu cau bat ky niem tin tan giao nao.

Ho goi no la Gateway Experience.

### CHEC Unit — Khong Gian Duoc Thiet Ke De "Ra Ngoai"

Dieu dau tien can noi la moi truong vat ly cua chuong trinh nay.

CHEC unit — viet tat cua "Controlled Holistic Environmental Chamber" — la nhung "o" den trong a am thanh, duoc thiet ke rieng cho moi hoc vien. Khong phai phong giam cam. Khong phai bong toi ap buc. La mot khong gian rieng tu hoan toan, noi khong co kich thich cam giac ben ngoai nao can thiep.

Tai sao quan trong?

Boi vi mot trong nhung kho khan lon nhat khi chuyen tu trang thai thuc sang trang thai Focus 10 hay cao hon la khong gian nao cu cu tim cach "neo" y thuc vao vat chat: tieng on, anh sang, nhiet do, cam giac ve vi tri co the — tat ca deu nha y thuc tro ve voi than xac. CHEC unit loai bo cang nhieu neo dau do cang tot.

Trong o nay, hoc vien nam xuong, deo tai nghe, bat dau audio Hemi-Sync. Tat ca nhung gi con lai la y thuc cua chinh ho.

### Chuong Trinh Hoc Tien Trien Tung Buoc

Gateway khong dua hoc vien vao Focus 21 ngay ngay dau. Chuong trinh duoc xay dung tu thap len cao, tu quen den la:

**Buoc 1 — Hoi Tam**: Truoc khi bat cu dieu gi xay ra, hoc vien hoc cach "hoi tat" vao mot diem. Khong phai to mo, khong phai ky vong — chi la hien dien hoan toan. Day la ki nang kho nhat vi nao nguoi ta quen song voi 40 luong kich thich cung mot luc.

**Buoc 2 — Relaxation Cot Loi**: Hoc vien hoc cach thu gian co y thuc tung bo phan co the ma khong roi vao giac ngu. Day la su khac biet nen tang giua Hemi-Sync va thien dinh thong thuong: muc tieu la giu tam tri hoan toan tinh tao trong khi than the vao trang thai ngu.

**Buoc 3 — Focus 10**: Lan dau tien co the trai nghiem "mind awake, body asleep". Nhieu hoc vien bao cao cam giac mat di ranh gioi vat ly cua than the — khong la nhat thiet di kem voi cam giac sot ruot hay hoang man, ma chi don gian la... khong con cam nhan duoc chan tay nua.

**Buoc 4 — Tiep Xuc Noi Tam**: O Focus 12, hoc vien bat dau lam quen voi "day truyen thong noi" — kha nang nhan thong tin khong qua cac giac quan binh thuong. Thuong bieu hien qua hinh anh hoc tu tuong xuat hien dot ngot khong tu y.

**Buoc 5 — OBE Co Chu Dich**: Chi den giai doan nay hoc vien moi thu de co trai nghiem OBE — va duoc dac cach khac hoan toan voi "co gang OBE" theo kieu chuyen thong. Ho khong "co gang tach xuat". Ho xu ly quy trinh thu gian, dat o Focus 12 hay 15, va cho phep moi thu xay ra tu nhien.

### Khi "Giac Mo Sang Suot" La Buoc Trung Gian

Mot dieu thu vi Monroe ghi lai: nhieu hoc vien — truoc khi dat duoc OBE day du — co nhung "lucid dream" (giac mo sang suot, biet minh dang ngu) cuc ki ro rang va co the dieu khien.

Monroe coi day la buoc dem trung gian, khong phai dich den cuoi cung. Trong giac mo sang suot, y thuc van dang lam viec qua nen quy chieu cua giac mo — mot loai thuc tai duoc xay dung chu yeu tu ky uc va chuong trinh tam ly cua chinh nguoi mo. Trong OBE day du, y thuc hoat dong doc lap khoi bat ky nen quy chieu nao — khong gian, vat the, cac thuc the gap phai tuan theo nhung quy luat khac voi quy luat tam ly ca nhan.

Su khac biet nay — co ve nhu khi niem — that ra la su khac biet nen tang khi su dung hai loai trai nghiem nhu nguon thong tin.

### Khi CIA Doc Bao Cao Cua Monroe

Co mot chi tiet lich su dang chu y: nam 1983, CIA da uy quyen cho Lien Doan Phan Tich Vien Quan Su Hoa Ky viet mot bao cao ky luong ve Gateway Program cua Monroe Institute.

Bao cao do — duoc giai mat nam 2003 va co the tim thay cong khai tren Internet — kha chuong Hemi-Sync va cach no tac dong len trang thai y thuc o muc ly thuyet. Nha phan tich viet bao cao, Maj. Wayne M. McDonnell, mo ta no la "chuyen gap go thu vi nhat voi mot chuong trinh dao tao ma toi tung gap".

Day khong co nghia CIA "xac nhan OBE" — bao cao di sau vao vat ly hoc cua am thanh va y thuc nhung khong ket luan gi ve OBE cu the. Nhung no cho thay co nhung nguoi trong he thong chinh phu My da coi cong viec cua Monroe du nghiem tuc de dau tu thoi gian nghien cuu.

### Dieu Chuong Trinh Day Roi Moi Chuong Trinh Day

Co mot dieu thiet thuc ma Gateway Experience mang lai cho hoc vien — ngoai kha nang OBE:

Hoc cach to chuc lai noi tam.

Nhieu nguoi song trong mot trang thai y thuc roi loan co man: suy nghi chay dua lien tuc, cam xuc khong duoc xu ly, su chu y nhay tung chut mot. Gateway — du hoc vien co OBE hay khong — day ho cach lam chu su chu y, cach "hoi tam" y thuc, cach cho phep trang thai thay doi ma khong mat kiem soat.

Day la ky nang co ich trong bat ky boi canh nao cua cuoc song: giao tiep sau hon, lang nghe ton tai hon, ra quyet dinh tu trang thai binh tinh hon.

Monroe khong xay dung Gateway chi cho nhung nguoi muon OBE. Ong xay dung no cho bat ky ai muon hieu y thuc cua minh hon — du dich den cuoi cung la gi.
