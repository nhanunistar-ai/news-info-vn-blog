Ban dang o trang muc luc cua series _Far Journeys_ — cuon sach thu hai trong bo ba cua Robert A. Monroe.

> Co nhung cuon sach mo ta trai nghiem cua mot nguoi. Cung co nhung cuon sach dung trai nghiem do de dat cau hoi ve ban chat cua tat ca chung ta. _Far Journeys_ thuoc nhom thu hai.

### Robert Monroe la ai?

Robert Allan Monroe (1915-1995) la mot doanh nhan va nha nghien cuu nguoi My, nguoi dau tien dua trai nghiem xuat hon (out-of-body experience — OBE) ra khoi pham tru huyen bi va dua vao nghien cuu co he thong.

Truoc khi tro thanh "nha tham hiem y thuc" noi tieng nhat the ky 20, Monroe la giam doc dieu hanh cua mot chuoi dai phat thanh. Nam 1958, o tuoi 43, ong bat dau co nhung trai nghiem bat ngo: y thuc ong tach khoi co the vat ly trong khi van hoan toan tinh tao va nhan thuc. Khong co giao duc than hoc, khong theo mot truyen thong tam linh nao — ong don gian la mot nguoi binh thuong bi "say nham" vao mot thu gi do rat khac voi nhung gi khoa hoc thoi do mo ta.

Cuon sach dau tien, _Journeys Out of the Body_ (1971), ghi lai nhung nam dau tien cua hanh trinh do: hoang mang, sot ruot, dan dan hinh thanh mot phuong phap nghien cuu. Den _Far Journeys_ (1985), moi thu da khac di hoan toan.

### Far Journeys la gi?

_Far Journeys_ khong phai la nhung cau chuyen OBE tiep theo cua mot nguoi ham vui. No la buoc chuyen tu trai nghiem ca nhan sang nghien cuu tap the, tu khao sat ca nhan sang xay dung mot he thong co the tai lap va giang day.

Den nam 1985, Monroe da:

- Thanh lap Monroe Institute tai Virginia, mot trung tam nghien cuu y thuc voi day du trang thiet bi
- Phat trien cong nghe Hemi-Sync - phuong phap dung am thanh de dong bo hai ban cau nao va tao ra cac trang thai y thuc khac nhau co the tai lap
- Dao tao hang tram nguoi tham gia vao "doi Explorer" - nhom tinh nguyen vien co kha nang OBE duoc giam sat va ghi am
- Xay dung he thong "Focus Level" mo ta cac trang thai y thuc tu 10 den 27 va xa hon nua

_Far Journeys_ ghi lai qua trinh do va nhung gi Monroe cung doi Explorer kham pha: ban do cac coi phi vat ly, cac loai thuc the khac nhau, khai niem "Loosh" (nang luong cam xuc), va ket thuc bang chuong gut long nhat — "The Gathering" — noi Monroe nhin thay hang trieu thuc the phi vat ly tu tap xung quanh Trai Dat cho mot su kien gi do sap xay ra.

### Cach doc series nay

Series nay khong dich nguyen van _Far Journeys_. Thay vao do, moi bai viet:

- Tom luoc mach chinh cua tung chuong
- Giai thich cac khai niem Monroe su dung bang ngon ngu de hieu
- Noi trai nghiem cua Monroe voi nhung cau hoi lon hon: y thuc la gi? Cai chet la gi? Con nguoi o dau trong vu tru?
- Chi ra nhung diem can thi truong khi tiep can noi dung nay

Tien do kham pha se di tu giao du, he thong, doi Explorer (Phan I: Near Reaches) den nhung cuoc du hanh ngay cang xa va bi an hon (Phan II: Far Reaches).

### Phan I: Near Reaches — Nhung Coi Gan

Phan I tap trung vao nen tang: Monroe lam the nao de bien OBE thanh mot he thong nghien cuu co the giang day va tai lap?

1. **Chuong 1 — Nhung Chuyen Du Hanh Cu**: Tro lai voi nhung canh quan phi vat ly quen thuoc — cac thuc the "dia phuong", khai niem "Rote", va nhung gi Monroe da biet truoc khi bat dau nghien cuu co he thong.

2. **Chuong 2 — Hemi-Sync**: Cong nghe dong bo nao la gi? Binaural beats tac dong the nao? Va tai sao am thanh co the "mo cua" vao cac trang thai y thuc khac nhau?

3. **Chuong 3 — Gateway Program**: Chuong trinh Gateway Experience duoc thiet ke nhu the nao? Nhung buoc dau tien cua mot hoc vien khi buoc vao CHEC unit la gi?

4. **Chuong 4 — Doi Explorer**: Ai la nhung nguoi tien phong? Ho kham pha duoc gi khi duoc dao tao va giam sat? Ban do cac Focus Level hinh thanh nhu the nao?

5. **Chuong 5 — Nhung Lien Ket Moi**: Monroe gap "INSPEC" - mot loai thuc the phi vat ly khac hoan toan voi nhung gi ong gap truoc day. Giao tiep vuot ngoai ngon ngu nghe co ly nhu the nao?

6. **Chuong 6 — Buoc Chuyen**: Chuong cau noi truoc khi moi thu tro nen xa hon, la hon va kho tin hon nhieu.

---

### Phan II: Far Reaches — Nhung Coi Xa

Phan II la phan nhiều nguoi nho den nhat khi noi ve _Far Journeys_ — va cung la phan chia re nguoi doc nhat.

7. **Chuong 7 — Ban Do Cac Coi**: So do chi tiet cac "vung" phi vat ly — Focus 27, coi dem, cac he thuc tai khac nhau.

8. **Chuong 8 — Diem Tiep Xuc**: Gap go "du khach" tu cac he thuc tai khac — khong phai nguoi Trai Dat — va nhung gi ho muon noi.

9. **Chuong 9 — Lo Trinh Cau Vong**: Hanh lang giua cac chieu ton tai, duoc mo ta nhu mot day quang pho cua y thuc.

10. **Chuong 10 — Nguoi Ban Moi**: Monroe gap "nguoi ban" phi vat ly tro thanh huong dan vien lau dai — va khai niem "Ithaca" — diem quy ve.

11. **Chuong 11 — Nhiem Vu Giai Cuu**: Nhung linh hon mac ket, khong biet minh da chet, va cach Monroe cung doi Explorer giup ho tien ve phia truoc.

12. **Chuong 12 — Loosh**: Chuong gay tranh cai nhat. "Loosh" la gi? Trai Dat co phai la "trang trai" thu hoach nang luong cam xuc? Va tai sao Monroe sau do dieu chinh lai cach hieu cua minh?

13. **Chuong 13 — Lieu Phap Soc**: Nhung cuoc gap go pha vo moi mo hinh tu duy cu ve thien duong, dia nguc va vu tru.

14. **Chuong 14 — Bai Hoc Don Gian**: "Tat ca la mot" — khong phai khau hieu, ma la trai nghiem truc tiep.

15. **Chuong 15 — Ke Hoach Da Hua**: Co mot "ke hoach" lon hon cho nhan loai khong? Monroe nghi rang co.

16. **Chuong 16 — Cuoc Tap Hop**: Dinh diem. Hang trieu thuc the dang doi cho. Trai Dat dang duoc nhin ngam boi mot thu gi rat lon.
